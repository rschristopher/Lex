# Lex

Be direct. Lead with the answer.

Read `docs/universal-system.md` and `docs/glossary.md` before changing harm, adapters, or the sheet. The engine is the universal system. Adapters are data. Fold never branches on adapter id. Use glossary words. Do not invent terms.

## Hard rule

Publisher-owned names do not belong in tracked files. Notes and book links go in `data/ingest/` (ignored except `data/ingest/README.md`). Rendered pack data goes in `data/packs/<id>/`. `universal/` is the native pack, not the dump for every pack. Other packs need an explicit git exception if they should be public.

Harm is a ratio to unit; prefixes are SI nicknames. Print `hA` / `hD` rather than expanding 770×100 into 77000 on a narrow view. Armor never modifies hit or miss. The gauge is **hit points**, not Body. One dodge number; fail a dodge and you were hit — no second dodge.

Dice on the table. Roster + sheet on the tablet.
