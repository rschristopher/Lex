# Bake-off stories

Story beat files for `lex bakeoff` (format: the docstring at the top of `lex/bakeoff.py`), each next to the
public-domain text its quotes come from. `lex bakeoff check` and `tests/test_bakeoff.py` check every quote
against that text. Numbers read from a story carry `"estimate": true` and a note saying how they were read.

Candidates, mappings, crosswalks, and results are not kept here.
